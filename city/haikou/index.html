Hidden-gem food city · Hainan

Haikou

Coconut fragrance, tender chicken, and cooling sweets turn tropical island cooking light and easy.

Haikou gathers Hainan’s island produce into an everyday table. Wenchang chicken is silky and clean-tasting, Hainanfen layers sweet, sour, and savory notes, qingbuliang cools the afternoon with coconut milk and grains, and coconut rice steams sticky rice inside pure tropical aroma.

Tropical coconut Refreshing noodles Island slow food
01

What to eat in Haikou

  1. 01

    Wenchang Chicken

    Poached chicken is sliced with glossy skin and tender meat, then brightened by tangy, garlicky dipping sauces.

  2. 02

    Hainanfen

    Fine rice noodles are dressed with gravy, pickles, peanuts, and shredded meat for a sweet-sour-savory mix.

  3. 03

    Qingbuliang

    Coconut milk, mung beans, grains, dates, and fruit make a cooling Hainan dessert bowl.

  4. 04

    Coconut Rice

    Sticky rice steams with coconut flesh and juice, becoming soft, lightly sweet, and deeply aromatic.

02

Eat like a local

Eat Wenchang chicken and Hainanfen at lunch, cool down with qingbuliang, and finish with coconut rice as a gentle sweet.
