Hidden-gem food city · Henan

Luoyang

Soup runs through every meal as sour heat, wheat, and ancient banquet traditions meet.

Luoyang begins the day over soup: beef, mutton, bufan, and pepper soups are all part of the morning rhythm. The celebrated Water Banquet turns soup-rich dishes into an old-capital ceremony, while tangy fermented noodles and crisp-bottomed guotie reveal the city’s everyday wheat traditions.

Soup-house mornings Bright sour heat Ancient banquets

What to eat in Luoyang

  1. 01

    Luoyang Water Banquet

    Soup-rich dishes arrive in sequence, balancing lively sour heat with the formal rhythm of an old-capital feast.

  2. 02

    Hulatang

    A thick pepper soup with gluten and vegetables, served as a warming, spicy Luoyang breakfast.

  3. 03

    Xiaojie Guotie

    Crescent dumplings fry to a golden base while keeping their filling juicy and their upper wrappers tender.

  4. 04

    Jiangmian Tiao

    Noodles soften in a lightly fermented sour broth with soybeans, green peas, and chopped vegetables.

02

Eat like a local

Visit soup shops early, share the many courses of a Water Banquet, and leave room for guotie and fermented noodles later.
