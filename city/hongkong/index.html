Classic food city · Hong Kong SAR

Hong Kong

Springy wonton noodles, silky milk tea, and warm bakery treats set the rhythm of Hong Kong.

Hong Kong brings Cantonese craft and the East-meets-West culture of cha chaan teng cafes to one table. Wonton noodles balance clear broth with springy noodles and shrimp fillings, pineapple buns accompany afternoon milk tea, and freshly baked egg waffles perfume the streets.

Cha chaan teng culture Fresh noodle soups Street-side baking
01

What to eat in Hong Kong

  1. 01

    Wonton Noodles

    Springy fine egg noodles and plump shrimp-and-pork wontons meet a light, savory broth.

  2. 02

    Pineapple Bun with Butter

    A soft bun with a crackly sweet crust holds a slab of cold butter; despite its name, it contains no pineapple.

  3. 03

    Egg Waffles

    Egg batter bakes into connected little bubbles with crisp edges and soft centers, best eaten straight from the iron.

  4. 04

    Hong Kong Milk Tea

    Strong filtered black tea blended with evaporated milk makes a smooth, full-bodied drink, served hot or iced.

02

Eat like a local

Try wonton noodles in the morning, pair a buttered pineapple bun with afternoon milk tea, and eat egg waffles freshly baked.
