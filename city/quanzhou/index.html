Hidden-gem food city · Fujian

Quanzhou

A Maritime Silk Road city where gentle seafood, ginger warmth, noodles, and zongzi meet.

Quanzhou cooking carries both Minnan seafood clarity and an old overseas-Chinese rhythm. Misua paste is silky and warming, ginger duck slowly draws depth from old ginger and aromatics, roast pork zongzi is hearty and compact, and runbing wraps vegetables, seafood, meat, and sweet peanut powder into one fresh bite.

Minnan freshness Old-school snacks Ginger warmth
01

What to eat in Quanzhou

  1. 01

    Misua Paste

    Fine wheat noodles cook into a smooth soup, often topped with fried pork, oysters, or braised garnishes.

  2. 02

    Ginger Duck

    Duck is braised with old ginger, sesame oil, and spices until warming, aromatic, and deeply seasoned.

  3. 03

    Roast Pork Zongzi

    Sticky rice wraps roast pork, mushrooms, dried shrimp, and other fillings, then pairs well with sweet chili sauce.

  4. 04

    Runbing

    Thin wrappers fold vegetables, seafood, shredded meat, and peanut sugar into a fresh, lightly sweet roll.

02

Eat like a local

Make misua paste breakfast, save ginger duck for a slower dinner, and graze the old city in small snack stops.
