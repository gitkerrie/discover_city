Hidden-gem food city · Macao SAR

Macao

Caramelized egg tarts and fragrant spices connect Macanese cooking with Chinese bakery traditions.

Macao brings together Cantonese flavors, Portuguese-inspired baking, and Macanese home cooking. Egg tarts offer blistered custard and flaky pastry, minchi turns minced meat and potatoes into comfort food, African chicken layers aromatic spices, and almond cookies provide a crumbly sweet finish.

Macanese cooking Layered spices Crumbly bakery treats
01

What to eat in Macao

  1. 01

    Portuguese-style Egg Tarts

    Flaky pastry cradles silky custard with caramelized spots, especially delicate while still warm.

  2. 02

    Minchi

    Minced meat, diced potatoes, and onion are cooked into a savory Macanese staple, often served with rice and a fried egg.

  3. 03

    African Chicken

    Chicken meets a rich sauce of chilies, garlic, and aromatic spices, with recipes varying between kitchens.

  4. 04

    Almond Cookies

    Mung bean flour forms a crumbly cookie enriched with almonds for a fragrant, nutty bite.

02

Eat like a local

Eat egg tarts warm, save a proper meal for minchi or African chicken, and sample almond cookies before choosing a souvenir box.
