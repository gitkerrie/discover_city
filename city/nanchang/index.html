Hidden-gem food city · Jiangxi

Nanchang

Cool mixed rice noodles, warm crock soups, chile, and rice aroma light up everyday Jiangxi flavor.

Nanchang food lives in rice noodles, clay crocks, and wok-fired street meals. Banfen opens the morning with soy sauce, preserved radish, and chili oil, crock soup builds a gentle savory base, fried rice noodles bring wok heat, and steamed pork with rice powder turns meat and grain into something soft and fragrant.

01

What to eat in Nanchang

  1. 01

    Nanchang Banfen

    Rice noodles are blanched, then mixed with soy sauce, chili oil, preserved radish, and scallions for a bright, punchy breakfast.

  2. 02

    Earthenware Pot Soup

    Small crocks slowly steam meat, egg, mushrooms, or other ingredients into a clear, warming soup.

  3. 03

    Nanchang Fried Rice Noodles

    Rice noodles meet high wok heat, soy aroma, chile, and smoky breath-of-the-wok flavor.

  4. 04

    Steamed Pork with Rice Powder

    Pork belly is coated in seasoned rice powder and steamed until soft, rich, and deeply fragrant.

02

Eat like a local

Start with banfen and crock soup for breakfast, then save fried noodles and steamed pork for a more chile-forward evening.
