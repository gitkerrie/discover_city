Hidden-gem food city · Henan

Kaifeng

Soup-bun steam meets night-market light, keeping old Bianjing flavors vividly alive.

Kaifeng cooking connects formal Henan banquet traditions with one of China’s liveliest night-market cultures. Soup buns prize thin wrappers and abundant broth, tongzi chicken brings aromatic master-stock depth, while mutton soup and peanut candy carry the day from savory warmth to a crisp sweet finish.

Old Bianjing flavors Night-market energy Broth and master stock
01

What to eat in Kaifeng

  1. 01

    Kaifeng Soup Buns

    Fine pleats hold pork and hot broth inside a thin wrapper; open, sip, then season with vinegar.

  2. 02

    Tongzi Chicken

    A whole chicken is cooked in aromatic master stock until golden, firm-textured, and deeply savory.

  3. 03

    Mutton Soup

    Mutton and bones simmer into a warming broth lifted with scallion, cilantro, and pepper.

  4. 04

    Peanut Candy

    Roasted peanuts and sugar are pounded and shaped into a crisp, fragrant sweet that finishes cleanly.

Eat like a local

Take soup buns slowly while hot, try tongzi chicken and mutton soup by day, then finish a night-market walk with peanut candy.
