Hidden-gem food city · Yunnan

Dali

Toasted milk fans, crisp Xizhou flatbreads, and warming rice noodles reveal the flavors of the Cangshan-Erhai region.

Dali food unfolds through Bai dairy traditions and rice-and-wheat snacks. Rushan cheese turns fragrant over heat, Xizhou baba comes sweet or savory, Weishan pork rice noodles marry tender meat with a soft rice base, and cooked aquatic greens reveal a lighter side of the table. Explore these tastes across Dali Old Town, Xizhou, and Weishan.

Bai dairy traditions Fresh lake-region greens Old-town rice and wheat
01

What to eat in Dali

  1. 01

    Grilled Rushan Cheese

    Thin sheets of milk cheese soften over heat and roll around sweet fillings such as rose syrup, with a gently tangy dairy aroma.

  2. 02

    Xizhou Baba

    A round flatbread with a browned crust, filled with pork and scallions or a sweet sugar-and-rose mixture.

  3. 03

    Weishan Pork Ersi

    Slow-cooked pork tops soft rice-cake noodles in meat broth, brightened with scallions and pickled vegetables.

  4. 04

    Stir-Fried Aquatic Greens

    Locally called shuixingyanghua, these tender cooked greens are a seasonal vegetable in Bai cuisine.

02

Eat like a local

Eat cheese and flatbreads hot, give Xizhou and Weishan separate stops, and order aquatic greens when they are in season.
