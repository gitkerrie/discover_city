Hidden-gem food city · Yunnan

Lijiang

Crisp Naxi flatbreads and refreshing chickpea jelly set a delicious pace through the highland old town.

Lijiang connects Naxi food traditions with the Mosuo tables of Ninglang and Lugu Lake. Layered baba flatbreads contrast with chickpea jelly served cool or warm; rice cakes cooked with chicken provide a hearty regional meal, while rose pastries bring a sweet Yunnan finish. Cured pork ribs offer another direction for exploring local restaurants.

Naxi traditions Beans and rice cakes Highland old town
01

What to eat in Lijiang

  1. 01

    Lijiang Baba

    Oiled dough is folded and cooked into a layered flatbread, with sweet or savory versions and a crisp, fragrant surface.

  2. 02

    Chickpea Jelly

    Fine-textured chickpea jelly is served chilled or pan-fried with chili, vinegar, and garlic.

  3. 03

    Mosuo Rice-Cake Chicken

    A Ninglang and Lugu Lake regional dish pairing chicken with soft, chewy erkuai rice cakes that absorb the cooking juices.

  4. 04

    Rose Pastries

    Flaky pastry wraps a sweet edible-rose filling in a Yunnan bakery favorite also found throughout Lijiang.

02

Eat like a local

Sample flatbreads and jelly in small portions, share rice-cake chicken at a proper meal, and explore more Mosuo flavors around Lugu Lake.
