Classic food city · Liaoning

Dalian

Crisp-edged menzi, salted fish with corn cakes, and seafood tell the everyday story of a Liaodong port.

Dalian combines Liaodong seafood with northern home cooking. Menzi starch jelly is pan-fried and dressed with sesame sauce and garlic; salted fish meets hearty corn cakes; boiled sea crab keeps the focus on the catch; and homemade dumplings round out a family meal. Spanish-mackerel dumplings offer another direction for exploring local fillings.

Liaodong seafood Garlicky street snacks Northern home cooking
01

What to eat in Dalian

  1. 01

    Dalian Menzi

    Starch-jelly cubes are pan-fried until crisp-edged, then dressed with sesame sauce and garlic for a soft-centered street snack.

  2. 02

    Salted Fish with Corn Cakes

    Savory salted fish pairs with cornmeal cakes, balancing concentrated fish flavor with gently sweet grain.

  3. 03

    Boiled Sea Crab

    Simply boiled crab highlights its natural sweetness, with ginger-vinegar dipping sauce and seasonal availability.

  4. 04

    Homestyle Dumplings

    Dumplings with meat, vegetable, or seafood fillings are boiled and served hot as a familiar centerpiece of family meals.

02

Eat like a local

Snack on menzi, eat salted fish together with its corn cakes, and confirm seafood weights and prices; choose dumpling fillings from the actual menu.
