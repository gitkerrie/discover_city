Hidden-gem food city · Shanxi

Taiyuan

Vinegar brightness, chewy noodles, and steaming mutton broth show the sturdy side of Shanxi cooking.

Taiyuan is an entry point to Shanxi noodles and old-city flavors. Tounao is warming and aromatic, knife-cut noodles show off firm edges and soft centers, guoyourou balances tender pork with bright vinegar, and yangzage wakes the morning with hot offal soup, pepper, and herbs.

Shanxi noodles Old-city vinegar Mutton-broth warmth
01

What to eat in Taiyuan

  1. 01

    Tounao

    A warming soup of huangjiu, mutton, lotus root, and medicinal aromatics, long tied to Taiyuan breakfasts.

  2. 02

    Knife-Cut Noodles

    Thick-edged noodle slices land springy in broth, especially lively with meat sauce, vinegar, and chili.

  3. 03

    Guoyourou

    Pork slices are oil-blanched, then stir-fried with wood ear and vegetables in a bright vinegar-leaning sauce.

  4. 04

    Yangzage

    Sliced mutton offal in hot broth gets lifted by pepper, scallion, and cilantro.

02

Eat like a local

Wake early for tounao or yangzage, eat knife-cut noodles at lunch, and let vinegar lead when ordering guoyourou.
