Hidden-gem food city · Jiangsu

Changzhou

Sesame-scented flatbreads and a bowl of vegetarian noodles introduce daily life beside the Grand Canal.

Changzhou rewards visits to noodle shops, bakeries, and neighborhood tofu makers. Layered sesame cakes contrast with humble soybean-pulp cakes, Hengshanqiao tofu brings sweet-savory flavor, and Tianning Temple noodles offer vegetable toppings in warming broth. Tofu soup, fine silk noodles, and crab-filled soup buns invite further exploration.

What to eat in Changzhou

  1. 01

    Changzhou Sesame Cakes

    Oval, sesame-covered flatbreads bake into crisp layers, with sweet and savory versions to accompany soup or congee.

  2. 02

    Douzhai Soybean Cakes

    Also called douzha cakes, these small soybean-pulp cakes are a modest local snack with a gentle bean aroma.

  3. 03

    Hengshanqiao Sweet-Marinated Tofu

    Seasoned tofu carries a sweet-savory glaze and works as a small side dish or part of a shared meal.

  4. 04

    Tianning Temple Vegetarian Noodles

    Fine noodles meet vegetable toppings and hot broth for a light meal around the Tianning Temple area.

02

Eat like a local

Start with warm sesame cakes, then visit a noodle shop; share small tofu dishes and leave room for tofu soup or local soup buns.
