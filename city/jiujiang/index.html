Hidden-gem food city · Jiangxi

Jiujiang

Wok-fragrant rice noodles and bold Jiangxi cooking bring everyday warmth to the foot of Mount Lu.

Jiujiang lies beside the Yangtze and near Poyang Lake, bringing freshwater traditions together with aromatic Jiangxi cooking. Fried rice noodles introduce the daily rhythm, while rice-powder pork, beer-braised duck, and quick-fried beef show the regional restaurant repertoire. Seek out radish pastries, tea cakes, and Xiushui shaozi for more local discoveries.

What to eat in Jiujiang

  1. 01

    Jiujiang Fried Rice Noodles

    Rice noodles meet vegetables, shredded meat, and soy seasoning over fierce heat for a springy, fragrant plate.

  2. 02

    Steamed Pork with Rice Powder

    Seasoned rice powder coats pork slices and absorbs their juices as they steam into a tender Jiangxi comfort dish.

  3. 03

    Beer-Braised Duck

    Duck slowly cooks with beer and aromatics into a savory sauce, often lifted with fresh chili.

  4. 04

    Stir-Fried Beef

    Thin beef slices are quickly tossed with chilies and garlic, a lively example of Jiangxi home-style cooking.

02

Eat like a local

Begin with fried rice noodles, share Jiangxi dishes at a proper meal, and leave an old-town walk for radish pastries and tea cakes.
