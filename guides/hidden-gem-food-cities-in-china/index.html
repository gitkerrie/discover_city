BEYOND THE CLASSICS

Nineteen hidden-gem food cities

The most memorable food trip is often one stop beyond the obvious itinerary. These cities reward travelers with distinctive local ingredients, techniques, and everyday eating rituals.
01 · Guangdong

Chaozhou

Pure flavors and aromatic braises meet rice-cake craft and glowing charcoal.

Fresh Beef Hot Pot · Oyster Omelet · Master-Stock Goose · Red Peach Rice Cake
02 · Guangxi

Liuzhou

Sour, hot, savory, and steaming flavors converge in one audacious bowl of noodles.

Luosifen · Sanjiang Oil Tea · Stuffed Tofu · Bamboo Rice
03 · Jilin

Yanji

Icy noodles and glowing charcoal bring Korean-Chinese traditions together on the border.

Yanji Cold Noodles · Rice Sausage · Charcoal Skewers · Stone-Pot Bibimbap
04 · Zhejiang

Taizhou

Between mountain and sea, chewy rice snacks share the table with vivid seafood.

Fanfenyuan · Braised Hairtail · Pan-Fried Yellow Croaker · Steamed Blue Crab
05 · Xinjiang

Kashgar

Tandoor smoke and warm spice meet the bright fruit of the western oases.

Lamb Polu · Tandoor Samsa · Xinjiang Naan · Lamb Skewers
06 · Jiangsu

Yangzhou

Knife work, clear broths, and gentle flavors unfold over an unhurried morning tea.

Braised Shredded Tofu · Three-Dice Bao · Red-Braised Lion's Head · Yangzhou Fried Rice
07 · Jiangsu

Nanjing

Duck runs through every meal, while soup dumplings and sweet rice desserts soften the ancient capital.

Nanjing Salted Duck · Duck Blood Vermicelli Soup · Chicken-Broth Soup Dumplings · Red Bean Jiuniang Rice Balls
08 · Heilongjiang

Harbin

Sweet-sour pork, smoked sausage, and giant rye loaves supply serious warmth in the northern cold.

Guobaorou · Harbin Red Sausage · Dalieba Bread · Soy-Braised Pork Bones
09 · Fujian

Xiamen

Satay-peanut richness and sweet shellfish give Minnan snacks the scent of the sea.

Shacha Noodles · Tusundong · Oyster Omelet · Xiamen Fried Rice Vermicelli
10 · Yunnan

Kunming

Flowers, rice noodles, and a steam pot make the Spring City taste fresh and wonderfully untamed.

Crossing-the-Bridge Rice Noodles · Steam-Pot Chicken · Grilled Erkuai · Yunnan Flower Cake
11 · Guizhou

Guiyang

Fermented sour broth wakes the palate while toasted chilies build a deeper, fragrant heat.

Siwawa · Sour-Broth Beef · Douhua Noodles · Red-Braised Beef Rice Noodles
12 · Shandong

Jinan

Spring water, glossy sauces, and patient heat give Shandong flavor a generous capital.

Nine-Turn Pork Intestines · Bazirou · Tianmo · Sweet-Sour Carp
13 · Fujian

Fuzhou

Clear soups, lively sweet-sour notes, and mountain and sea meeting in a single clay jar.

Buddha Jumps Over the Wall · Fuzhou Fish Balls · Lychee Pork · Rouyan
14 · Guangxi

Nanning

Sour bamboo, fermented black beans, lemon, and chili turn southern humidity into appetite.

Laoyoufen · Lemon Duck · Suanye · Five-Colored Glutinous Rice
15 · Jiangxi

Nanchang

Cool mixed rice noodles, warm crock soups, chile, and rice aroma light up everyday Jiangxi flavor.

Nanchang Banfen · Earthenware Pot Soup · Nanchang Fried Rice Noodles · Steamed Pork with Rice Powder
16 · Hainan

Haikou

Coconut fragrance, tender chicken, and cooling sweets turn tropical island cooking light and easy.

Wenchang Chicken · Hainanfen · Qingbuliang · Coconut Rice
17 · Fujian

Quanzhou

A Maritime Silk Road city where gentle seafood, ginger warmth, noodles, and zongzi meet.

Misua Paste · Ginger Duck · Roast Pork Zongzi · Runbing
18 · Liaoning

Shenyang

Chicken racks, dumplings, smoked pork flatbread, and steaming wheat snacks define Northeast street-table joy.

Laobian Dumplings · Grilled Chicken Rack · Smoked Pork Flatbread · Shenyang Shaomai
19 · Shanxi

Taiyuan

Vinegar brightness, chewy noodles, and steaming mutton broth show the sturdy side of Shanxi cooking.

Tounao · Knife-Cut Noodles · Guoyourou · Yangzage
20 · Jiangsu

Xuzhou

Peppery soup, mutton-broth steam, and bold wok flavor power the nights of the Huaihai region.

Xuzhou Pepper Soup · Bazirou · Sesame Liangpi · Meat-Sauce Rice Noodles
21 · Henan

Luoyang

Soup runs through every meal as sour heat, wheat, and ancient banquet traditions meet.

Luoyang Water Banquet · Hulatang · Xiaojie Guotie · Jiangmian Tiao
22 · Henan

Kaifeng

Soup-bun steam meets night-market light, keeping old Bianjing flavors vividly alive.

Kaifeng Soup Buns · Tongzi Chicken · Mutton Soup · Peanut Candy
23 · Macao SAR

Macao

Caramelized egg tarts and fragrant spices connect Macanese cooking with Chinese bakery traditions.

Portuguese-style Egg Tarts · Minchi · African Chicken · Almond Cookies
